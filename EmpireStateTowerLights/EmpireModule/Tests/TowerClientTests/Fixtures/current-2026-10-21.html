Frequently Asked Questions

You can submit a lighting request through our Tower Lighting Request page. However, please note that the Empire State Building does not grant lightings for personal events (such as birthdays, anniversaries, or weddings), commercial events, political campaigns, or religious figures or events.

Yes, the Empire State Building live cam that faces the tower shows the lights every night, including the signature hourly sparkle. The colors change for holidays, causes and milestones, and the Upcoming Special Lights list further up this page says which ones are scheduled next.

The Empire State Building selects its tower light colors to celebrate holidays, events, partnerships, and special occasions throughout the year.
